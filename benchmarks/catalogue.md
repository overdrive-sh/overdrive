# Benchmark catalogue

One row per benchmark. The substrate, the statistics flag and the caveats of a run are in its
record's header; methodology version 1 applies to every row (see `methodology.md`).

| Family and benchmark | Boundary and arms | Workload matrix | Clock boundary | Result owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `cgroup-connect-hook` / `connect_hook_cost` | per-connect cost of the gateway `cgroup/connect4` program; arms `no-hook` (reference, gateway link detached, nothing attached on the cgroup) and `gateway-hook` | 1 profile; the gateway connect program the dataplane boot attaches; per replicate a fresh cgroup, bpffs pin directory and veth pair; 500 warm-up then 5,000 timed loopback connect-and-close (`SO_LINGER` 0); 30 replicates per arm | `hook_ns_per_connect`: the gateway program's kernel `run_time_ns` over `run_count` (BPF statistics), on `gateway-hook` only and absent on `no-hook`; `wall_ns_per_connect`: host `CLOCK_MONOTONIC_RAW` stamps around the timed loop divided by 5,000, whose delta contains the cgroup-BPF dispatch cost | public ingress gateway (`overdrive-gateway`, `overdrive-dataplane`) |
| `vm-lifecycle` / `vm_lifecycle_stages` | host-observable stages of a service VM workload from the operator deploy call to the cleanup calls after a stop, through in-process `serve`, `deploy` and `stop`; one arm `lifecycle`; guest-measured shutdown handling beside them | 5 profiles (`healthy-concurrency-1`, `healthy-concurrency-4`, `cold-cache`, `warm-cache`, `stop-grace-deadline`); per replicate a fresh `serve`, the guest kit workload image, 500m CPU and 128 MiB per VM; 30 replicates | host `CLOCK_MONOTONIC_RAW` stamps at the deploy and stop calls, the named lifecycle events, the `READY` beacon line and the `Stable` observation row: `admission_ns`, `vmm_launch_ns`, `guest_ready_ns`, `stable_ns`, `stop_submission_ns`, `shutdown_delivery_ns`, `guest_exit_ns`, `cleanup_calls_ns`; guest-measured `guest_shutdown_handling_ns` (table `guest-stages`): a duration `overdrive-init` measured on its own `CLOCK_MONOTONIC` with `Instant::elapsed`, at 1 ms resolution, never combined with a host stamp | VM driver and `serve` composition (`overdrive-worker`, `overdrive-control-plane`) |
| `dns-responder` / `dns_query_latency` | latency and throughput of the node-local DNS responder over UDP under scheduled-arrival load, with the responder's own per-datagram service time beside the clients' view; one arm `responder`; the responder is constructed directly with a recording `QueryObserver` | 4 profiles (`a-record-1-name-1-client-2000qps`, `a-record-1000-names-4-clients-8000qps`, `aaaa-no-data-100-names-1-client-2000qps`, `unknown-name-1-client-2000qps`), each stating its scheduled-arrival rate (qps) and duration (seconds); axes are query shape, name-table size, client socket count and arrival rate; per replicate a fresh network namespace, name table, responder and 200 warm-up queries per client socket; 30 replicates | `client_latency_ns` (table `client-observed`): host `CLOCK_MONOTONIC_RAW` from each query's scheduled send time to its matching reply, a late or wrong reply counted a failure; `client_throughput_qps`: correct replies over the schedule window; `resolver_service_ns` (table `resolver-internal`): the responder's receive-to-reply-sent interval on the injected monotonic `Clock::now`, recorded by the observer | DNS responder (`overdrive-control-plane`) |
| `mtls-path` / `mtls_hop` | cost of the host agent's mTLS hop against a plaintext baseline, closed-loop; arms `plaintext-baseline` (reference) and `composed-mtls`; egress: a plaintext dialler registered with the real intercept worker (`MtlsInterceptWorker`, `HostMtlsIntercept`, `HostMtlsEnforcement`) to a rustls mesh peer; ingress: `HostMtlsEnforcement` serving `Routed::Inbound` over a loopback leg C with an in-memory guest end (guest transport excluded) | 2 profiles (`egress`, `ingress`); per replicate a fresh rig, 10 warm-up then 100 sequential connections, one connection of 10,000 timed 64-byte round trips, one connection carrying one 256 MiB frame; 30 replicates per arm | host `CLOCK_MONOTONIC_RAW` stamps in the family's client: `connection_establishment_ns` (client socket connect, both arms), `first_byte_ns` (before connect to the first reply), `tail_latency_ns` (request to reply), `throughput_bytes_per_second` (256 MiB over first write to reply); `handshake_ns`: the duration of `MtlsEnforcement::enforce` through a timing decorator in front of the real enforcement, absent on `plaintext-baseline`; on ingress it starts when the enforcement accepts the connection, so it includes the wait for the client's `ClientHello` (client-side TLS work); on both profiles the TLS peer or client runs on the same host as the agent and may co-limit throughput and tail latency, so the numbers are not pure agent cost; resource cost over the throughput window, whole-host scope (no cgroup figure): `host_cpu_{user,system,irq,softirq}_ratio` (class tick delta over the delta of all CPU time from the aggregate `/proc/stat` line, user includes nice), `memory_bytes` (this process's VmRSS) and `socket_count` (its open socket descriptors) at the end of the window, `connection_concurrency` (the peak of client sessions the family held open during the window); egress `composed-mtls` only: `forward_userspace_cpu_ratio` and `return_splice_cpu_ratio` (the `mtls-out-fwd` and `mtls-out-ret` threads' share of the CPU the process's threads accrued in the window, from `/proc/self/task/*/stat`, leaving out the family's own `ovbm-` threads; the window carries the 256 MiB request one way, so the return pump is the light one; ticks are 10 ms, so the heavy-to-light ratio, recorded as the note `pump_heavy_to_light_lower_bound`, is a lower bound); both are absent on the baseline arm and on ingress (`asynchronous relay on shared runtime threads`) | mTLS enforcement and intercept worker (`overdrive-dataplane`, `overdrive-worker`) |
