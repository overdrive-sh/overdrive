# Node-local persistent volume protocol

Feature `node-local-persistent-volume` (GH #348). Decisions are numbered in `docs/feature/node-local-persistent-volume/feature-delta.md`: VD-2 (ADR-0311), VD-3 (ADR-0312), VD-8 (ADR-0317), VD-9 (ADR-0318) and SD-1 to SD-5 (ADR-0334, superseding a launch and node shutdown; approved 2026-11-18, modelled as one decision). The VD ADRs are **accepted** (the user approved the decisions on 2026-11-17); the model states their current decisions and nothing else. **SD-6 (the restart generation, ADR-0335) is modelled by its own spec, `specs/quint/restart-generation/` (user ruling A, 2026-11-18)**: this spec has no pre-launch outcome and no capacity flag, and it applies the restart budget rule by **importing** `specs/quint/restart-generation/restart_budget.qnt`, the one rule that spec owns and both models and both conformance replays check. The spec's identifiers (`storeUp`, `StoreAbsentBoot`, `store_absent_fails_typed_and_never_attaches`) keep their earlier spelling; in this document and the design, "store" in those names is the **volume pool**, the node-level LVM setup every volume is carved from.

The slice is the loop `overdrive deploy` can drive: declare a volume, create it at launch, attach it, survive crash, `workload restart`, stop, node reboot. Reclamation, erasure, a volume status row, a start gate and withdrawal are **out of scope for this slice** (ADR-0318, GH #211). A counterexample found against the larger design that contained them (model finding F-1) is recorded in the feature delta § Entry contract E3, not here.

## Abstraction

`node_volume.qnt`, module `node_volume`, is the state machine (`init`, `step`). One node, one workload (`replicas = 1`) with one declared volume, `NA` allocation ids (each minted once). The volume size is abstracted: "free >= size" is the boolean `spaceFree`. **The restart budget is the imported rule.** The state carries only what the rule reads: `cnt[a]` (the count at issue) and `old[a]` (issued for an earlier generation than the committed one) for the current row (`curA`, the highest allocation id with a row) and a `PENDING` launch; every other allocation's values are normalised to 0. `charged`, `exhausted`, the successor's count and the restart admission are `budgetCharged`, `budgetExhausted`, `budgetStartCnt` and `budgetMayRestart` of `restart_budget.qnt`; `exh` materialises `exhausted` for the conformance driver. `OpResume` and `WorkloadRestart` bump the generation (every issued allocation becomes `old`), so the successor is a generation replacement that inherits its predecessor's count and forgives a `Failed` predecessor; a crash restart carries the count + 1; a fresh placement starts at 0. There is no pre-launch failure and no pool-capacity flag: those belong to `specs/quint/restart-generation/`. That a superseded or lost launch is followed by exactly one placement for the generation is assumption A10 of the feature delta, owned there; this spec shows only what the volume needs of it: a superseded launch leaves no row, no charge and no VMM, its claim drains, the volume is kept, and the next `WlStart` (the ordinary rule) attaches the same volume with its data.

| Owner | Modelled as |
|---|---|
| volume pool | `storeUp` (composed or absent), `spaceFree`; one logical volume: `lv` (exists), `active` (false after creation until `ensure` activates it, and after a node reboot), identity `vid`, `nCreated` (volumes ever created); the guest's data as `dv` (model-only `data_version`), `maxDv` (ghost: the latest version ever written) |
| kernel claim (VD-8) | `claim`: 0 free, `a > 0` held by the VMM of allocation `a`. A killed VMM's claim lingers until `Drain`: the claim outlives the process (spike B2b). `claim_state` is the authentic arbiter; it reports `NotFound` for a missing or an inactive volume (no device node), see § Projection |
| launch path (VD-8, VD-9) | per allocation, control-plane memory: `ph` (supervision claimed, create-if-absent done, activated, claim reported Free, `LP_BOOT`: VMM spawned and holding the claim, guest not ready), `waited` (poll steps, bound `BOUND`). **A `PENDING` allocation stands for "a launch holds the lane"**: in production a launch in flight has no allocation row (the shim writes none before `driver.start` returns) and a stop acts only on `Running` rows, so the model's `PENDING` row is the launch itself, not a row an operator action can end. `GuestReady` is the boot race selecting `accept_ready`: it writes the `Running` row. `VmmSpawn` leaves the allocation `PENDING` (`LP_BOOT`). Actions `LaunchEnsureCreate`, `LaunchEnsureActivate` (two effects; a control-plane crash can interrupt after each), `LaunchUnavailable` (typed `VolumeUnavailable` after `ensure` began: the volume vanished) / `LaunchCmdFail` (typed `VolumeUnavailable`); a launch that cannot provide the volume at all (no pool composed, or no free extent for a missing volume) fails inside `WlStart` itself, in the same step: `ensure` is the launch's first step and fails typed there, so the allocation is never observably `PENDING` (a lane born raised is not failed there: it observes the signal first), `LaunchCheck`, `LaunchPoll`, `LaunchHeldFail` (typed `VolumeHeld`: the contract's `VolumeUnavailable { Held }`), `VmmSpawn` (takes the claim) / `VmmSpawnFail` (the VMM's own `O_EXCL` open is the atomic verdict) |
| lane signal (SD-1 to SD-5, ADR-0334) | `sup[a]`: the signal for allocation `a`'s launch, raised by `OpStop`, `WorkloadRestart` and `Shutdown` for a `PENDING` allocation; the abandonment rule is stated once in the header of `node_volume.qnt`. The one exception is a launch inside `ensure` (`LP_SUP`, `LP_CREATED`): it keeps `sup[a]` up and abandons in `LaunchEnsureActivate`. `WlStart` of a lane born after shutdown abandons before the volume step; every other `WlStart` starts with `sup[a]` false. Hazard `noInterrupt` removes the abandonment and the `not(sup)` guards. Ghosts: `afterSup`, `delivered`, `chg` (not projected). `CpCrash` and `NodeReboot` clear `sup` and `down` |
| workload-lifecycle | `WlStart`: mints a successor only when the workload is declared and not stopped, no allocation row is non-terminal (ADR-0109), no orphan VMM is alive (M7), `budgetMayRestart(charged, LIMIT, pendingGen)`, the id is the lowest unminted one; while `down` it is bounded to one evaluation by `dstart`; it does not wait for cleanup or claim release (ADR-0106). `StopAlloc` (a `Running` allocation that is stopped: reason `W_STOP`), `WorkloadRestart` (a `Running` allocation ends with `W_RESTART`; a `PENDING` one gets the signal and no row changes), `OpResume` (clears `stopped` and bumps the generation) |
| restart budget | the imported rule `restart_budget.qnt` over `cnt` / `old`: `charged` (the current row's count, +1 when it is `Failed`; every typed start failure and every crash of a `Running` allocation is a `Failed` row), `LIMIT` (production's ceiling + 1; abstracted), `exhausted` = BackoffExhausted terminal (`exh`) |
| environment (faults, each draws on `FB`; `Shutdown` is an operator-gated event, `OPS`, not a fault) | `VmmSigkill`, `DeviceSlow` (the dead VMM's in-flight I/O outlasts the launch bound), `LaunchCmdFail`, `CpCrash` (launch state lost, a spawned VMM keeps running until `Reclaim`, the existing reclamation), `NodeReboot` (volume inactive, VMMs gone, claims released). `Boot` (weakly fair; guard `down`) is the graceful path's next boot after `Shutdown`: it clears `down`, `sup` and `dstart` and applies the `CpCrash` loss to every launch the shutdown left unfinished (a `PENDING` allocation becomes `FAILED`/`W_LOST`, a spawned VMM becomes an orphan), without drawing on `FB`. `StoreAbsentBoot` and `PoolFull` are boot-time substrate facts (before anything exists). `Reclaim` kills any surviving orphan VMM (charging only a `Running` row) |

Not modelled: volume size and `SizeMismatch`, `NameOccupied` and foreign logical volumes (the hashed-name collision is accepted by ADR-0312), the network, identity, probes, removal of any volume (nothing deletes a volume in this slice), a volume pool that disappears after a volume exists (an exposure the feature delta bounds). Time is abstracted: a healthy drain finishes within the bound (`LaunchPoll` is enabled only when the holder is not a draining dead VMM, or after `DeviceSlow`).

## Decision, invariant, hazard

| Decision | Rule modelled | Invariants (`Safety` is the conjunction of all of them) | Hazard (`OFF`) and check |
|---|---|---|---|
| VD-8 (ADR-0317) | the launch waits for the claim, not the process; the kernel claim is the arbiter | `I_SINGLE_WRITER`; `I_NO_DRAIN_FAILURE_CHARGES_BUDGET` (no charged launch failure before the bound while only a draining claim was in the way) | `noWait`: the launch treats the VMM process exit as the claim free; `vol-hazard-wait` |
| VD-9 (ADR-0318) | `ensure` is create-if-absent then activate, idempotent under a crash between effects; failures are typed and charge the budget | `I_ENSURE_IDEMPOTENT_UNDER_CRASH`; `I_DATA_SURVIVES_RESTART` (shared with VD-2) | `recreate`: `ensure` creates unconditionally; target `I_VD9` = data survives and one volume; `vol-hazard-ensure` |
| VD-2 (ADR-0311) | the volume belongs to the workload and outlives stop, restart, crash and node reboot | `I_STOP_KEEPS_VOLUME`; `I_DATA_SURVIVES_RESTART` (the volume present is volume 1 with the latest `data_version`, and an attached volume is active) | `stopDeletes`: stop deletes the volume; `vol-hazard-stop` |
| SD-1 to SD-5 (ADR-0334) | only an operator stop/restart or node shutdown raises the lane signal; a launch with the signal abandons its claim wait and boot wait, `ensure` and the VMM create run to their end; a superseded launch delivers nothing (a `Terminated` row `LaunchSuperseded`, no `Running`, no `Failed`, no budget input, no VMM); superseded wins at the same observation point; shutdown signals every pending launch, including one whose lane opens after it | `I_SUPERSEDE_ABANDONS_WAITS`; `I_SUPERSEDED_LAUNCH_DELIVERS_NOTHING`; `I_SIGNAL_NEVER_LEAKS_ACROSS_ALLOCATIONS` | `noInterrupt` (SD-1 to SD-4): raising the signal only records it (no abandonment) and the `not(sup)` guards are gone, checked by `vol-hazard-interrupt` on `I_SUPERSEDE_ABANDONS_WAITS`. SD-1 to SD-5 are one decision (ADR-0334), so one hazard: shutdown's signal is established by construction and asserted by the run `shutdown_abandons_every_launch_including_one_admitted_late` |
| VD-3 (ADR-0312) | the backing choice (raw linear logical volume, extents reserved) | none: not a protocol rule | none |

A launch that fails typed never attaches a VMM: the typed-failure runs (`no_space…`, `launch_bound…`, `store_absent…`) assert `not(ran)`; there is no invariant for it, since no action can violate it.

Liveness (weak fairness of every system action family, finite faults, `FB < LIMIT`): `EveryStartLiveness` = a declared volume with a composed volume pool and free space eventually has a Running allocation (`StartLiveness`), and once an allocation has ended a Running one eventually exists again, i.e. a replacement attaches after the predecessor's claim drains (`ReplacementLiveness`); check `vol-live-start`. Teeth: with `FB = 3 = LIMIT` the same property is violated (three charged faults reach BackoffExhausted), verified during this run and reverted.

## Projection for the conformance driver

From the feature delta § "Observable projections the conformance driver reads", which is authoritative: `lv` and `active` are compared as `lv ∧ active ⇔ claim_state ≠ NotFound` (`VolumeBacking::claim_state`; an absent and an inactive volume both have no device node and report `NotFound`, so the state `lv ∧ ¬active`, which run `node_reboot_reactivates_and_data_survives` reaches, projects as `NotFound` and is otherwise observed by Tier-3 read-back; `SimVolumeBacking` reports `NotFound` while it models a volume as inactive); "claimed" from `claim_state` with `SimVolumeBacking` the named arbiter (paired Tier-3 test: `Held`, `Free`, inactive); `as` and `exhausted` from `ObservationStore::alloc_status_rows()` (`state`; a `BackoffExhausted { attempts }` terminal). Model-only (the driver does not project them; `charged` is observed through the rows as stated in the feature delta): `charged`, `sup`, `afterSup`, `delivered`, `chg`, `down` (the lane signal and its ghosts; the conformance driver raises the signal through the production handlers and asserts the owner's outcome through the projection table in the feature delta), `dstart` (a bound), `holder` (which allocation holds the claim), `dv` / `maxDv` / `vid` / `nCreated` (Tier-3 read-back), `storeUp` / `spaceFree` (fault-injection inputs), `ph`, `waited`, `alive`, `orphan`, `why`, `ran`, `faults`, `slow`, `drainFail`, `conf_step`. This projection rule is the conformance driver's comparison rule; it changes no spec file.

## Assumptions

Feature-delta assumptions:

| Assumption | How it appears in the model | Discharged by |
|---|---|---|
| A1 a held `O_EXCL` claim refuses a second claimant on a dm-linear device, through any node | `VmmSpawn` takes the claim atomically; `claim` is one value | spike run-031 (F2); Tier-3 `Held`/`Free` pairing test |
| A2 dm refuses to remove an open device | not relied on: nothing removes a volume in this slice | entry contract for GH #211 |
| A3 the claim and lock outlive the VMM process while in-flight I/O drains | `VmmSigkill`, `StopAlloc`, `WorkloadRestart` leave `claim = a` until `Drain`; `GuestWrite` is allowed while the dead holder drains | spike B2b (2.4 s); the model has one `claim` value because `claim_state` takes only the kernel claim, and a free claim implies a free lock for one open file description (`fs/file_table.c:461,468 @ v7.0`) |
| A4 LVM metadata operations are crash-atomic | each pool effect (create, activate) is one atomic action; a failed command leaves nothing partial | V-1 (passed: SIGKILLed `lvcreate`/`lvremove` and VMM kills leave a volume absent or present) |
| A5 a control-plane crash after `ensure` and before VMM spawn leaves the volume intact for the next launch | `CpCrash` loses launch state only; `lv`, `dv` persist | A4 |
| A6 the device persists what it acknowledges after a flush | out of model (power loss not tested) | none (stated in the feature delta) |

Modelling assumptions (this spec; each is a choice that can be revisited). Ids are M1 to M7 and M9:

| Id | Assumption | Discharged by |
|---|---|---|
| M1 | the volume is created inactive and activated by a second effect; a node reboot leaves the volume present and inactive. A conservative refinement: real `lvcreate` returns an active volume; an inactive one comes from a reboot or a killed `lvremove` | V-1 steps 2, 4 and 5 (passed) |
| M2 | a control-plane crash (and `Boot`, which applies the same loss) ends the allocation whose launch was lost `TERMINATED`/`W_LOST` (ADR-0355: boot's first step closes every `Pending` row; the allocation gains no state, the reason is the projection) and **charges nothing**; a spawned VMM stays running with its row `Running` until `VmmReclamation` kills it, then the row is `Failed` (`W_CRASH`, charged) and the claim drains. Exact, not conservative: the abandoned row is `Terminated`, not `Failed`, so nothing the restart budget counts is written; it is the current row, restartable and exempt from the ceiling, and the next `WorkloadLifecycle` tick mints a fresh allocation carrying its count unchanged (review finding M3, ADR-0355 item 2). Only a `Running` row killed by the crash, the reboot or the sweep is a `Failed` row that production charges | production writes the `Pending` row before the VMM exists and ends it with the abandoned row when the launch is lost (ADR-0353, ADR-0355; feature delta § Launches that end before running); `VmReclamation` reaps a surviving VMM's artifacts (feature delta § Required boundary scenarios, 5); DISTILL confirms it through the production owner path |
| M3 | a healthy drain finishes within the bound; `DeviceSlow` models the in-flight I/O that outlasts it | spike B2b; the 30 s bound is a policy constant (feature delta § Validation) |
| M4 | a launch that waited the whole bound ends the stuck-drain condition (`LaunchHeldFail` clears `slow`): the bound is the longest stuck request (ADR-0317). Without it, weak fairness lets every successor wait its bound and exhaust the allocation ids | spike B2b; the exposure "a claim that never drains" in the feature delta (ADR-0317 consequences); the 30 s bound is a policy value |
| M5 | (rewritten for SD) a launch with the lane signal abandons its waits and delivers nothing, in the step that reaches the observation point; `ensure` and the VMM create are never interrupted, so a launch signalled at `LP_CREATED` first finishes `ensure` and abandons in the step that ends it. | the Tier-3 and seeded-sim scenarios of feature delta § Required boundary scenarios 4 and 4a; assumptions A7 to A9 |
| M7 | a VMM that survived a control-plane crash (an orphan) is reclaimed before any launch starts: `WlStart` is disabled while an orphan is alive. A `Running` orphan already blocked minting through its non-terminal row; since `VmmSpawn` no longer writes the `Running` row, a booting orphan sits under a `Failed(W_LOST)` row (M2) and needs the explicit block. `Reclaim` kills any live orphan and charges only a `Running` row. Without it a successor polls on the live orphan, passes its bound and charges `Held`, a state production does not reach | feature delta § Required boundary scenarios 5 (`vm_reclamation_boot::converge` precedes reconcilers; a surviving VMM is reclaimed, its claim drains); DISTILL confirms through the production owner path. The first-pass counterexamples for this are in the modeler's return, not in `evidence/` |
| M9 | `dstart` is also reset by `CpCrash` and `NodeReboot` (not only `Shutdown` and `Boot`): it is read only while `down`, `Shutdown` resets it, so the reset merges equivalent states and changes no behaviour. `Boot` applies the full `CpCrash` loss, including marking a spawned VMM an orphan (a mid-boot launch's VMM must be reclaimed or its claim never drains). | state-space reduction; M7 |
| M6 | the restart budget is production's per-allocation count, applied by the imported rule `restart_budget.qnt`: a fresh placement starts at 0, a crash restart carries count + 1, a generation replacement (`OpResume`, `WorkloadRestart`) carries the predecessor's count unchanged and forgives a `Failed` predecessor; a lost or superseded launch has no row and charges nothing | production's per-allocation `restart_counts` (`workload_lifecycle.rs:51-52`, `:1316-1321`, `:1431`; ADR-0335 item 4); the rule's own runs and checks live in `specs/quint/restart-generation/` (RA9, R-1 = A), and the conformance replays of both specs compare `exhausted` against the owner |

The rule is owned by `specs/quint/restart-generation/`; this spec imports it and does not restate it. The run `superseded_launch_result_discarded` shows the generation replacement forgiving a `Failed` predecessor. `WorkloadRestart` has a branch for each current row it applies to: `Running` (ends), `PENDING` (signal), and `Failed` (an exhausted workload included: the row stays and the generation becomes pending, as in `restart_generation.qnt`); the run `restart_of_failed_row_forgives_the_failure` reaches the `Failed` branch: the successor carries the predecessor's count unchanged, so `charged` is 1, where a crash restart would carry count + 1.
